# Spider Man: Brand New Day
**I love Spider-Man: Brand New Day because it finally lets Peter Parker grow up and strip away the billionaire tech and Avengers safety nets. Four years after the world forgets who he is, he is completely broke, isolated, and fighting street-level crime on his own. It feels raw, grounded, and deeply emotional—turning away from a CGI spectacle into a personal story about loneliness, grief, and the hard work of reconnecting with the people around you. I rate this a 9 out of 10 because Mary Jane did not love Peter Parker in this movie."
